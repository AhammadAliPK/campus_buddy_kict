When the internet connection was disabled, all website links successfully opened Chrome but displayed the no connection because the websites could not load. However, the campus map, phone dialer and email application can still open because launching these applications does not need internet access. Some functions, such as loading online maps or sending emails, may still require connection. An internet connectivity check could be added to the `LinkOpener.open()` method before launching HTTP or HTTPS links to notify users when internet access is unavailable.
